Practical Agricultural Connectivity

How UMAKONEKTA Works

Designed specifically for rural Philippine agricultural conditions: zero digital wallet barriers, zero complex calendar integrations, and zero mandatory smartphone ownership.

1
Step 01

Discover Local Assets

Search machinery, solar pavements, or cold storage directly by municipality text without consuming data on heavy GPS maps.

check_circle Works in 2G / low-signal areas
2
Step 02

Review Terms & Spec

Check the equipment capacity, fuel inclusion terms, operator availability, and verified FCA cooperative ratings before scheduling.

check_circle Transparent municipal rates
3
Step 03

Submit Date String

Farmers simply type their harvest or planting window (e.g. "Nov 15-18 morning shift") avoiding brittle time-pickers.

check_circle Single-field plain text form
4
Step 04

Coordinate & Cultivate

The provider toggles "Approved" in their coordination dashboard. Both parties connect via direct call or cooperative dispatch desk.

check_circle No payment links or commissions

Empowering Farmers' Cooperatives & Associations (FCAs)

In many rural agrarian reform communities, elder farmers may not own smartphones or have consistent data. UMAKONEKTA solves this through Digital Cooperative Proxies:

verified_user

FCA Leader Booking Proxies

Accredited cooperative secretaries can log in and submit requests on behalf of up to 200 registered cooperative members with a single account.

notification_important

Crop Calendar Pre-Booking

Plan entire seasonal planting windows weeks in advance, avoiding harvest-time equipment crunches caused by sudden typhoon patterns.

cell_wifi

PWA Offline Mode Caching

Browsed listings remain accessible on your mobile phone even when you walk deep into the fields where cell towers cannot reach.
