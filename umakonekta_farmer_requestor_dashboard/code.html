How Offline Settlement Works

UMAKONEKTA does not require digital bank transfers or GCash balances. Settle directly with your cooperative:

1
Field Inspection: The certified operator completes the harvest or spray pass on your designated hectare lot.
2
Palay Weighing Ledger: Physical tally sheet is signed by you and the operator right on the paddy dike.
3
Co-op Clearing: Pay via cash upon sale, or deduct equivalent wet/dry palay bags at the Talavera Co-op Depot.
