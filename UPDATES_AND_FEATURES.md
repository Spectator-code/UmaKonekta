# UMAKONEKTA — Updates & New Features List

### 🧑‍🌾 1. Farmer Features & Updates
- **Quick Request Farm Machinery Modal**: Book Combine Harvesters, 4WD Heavy Tractors, or Drones with parcel sector selection and estimated rates.
- **Automated Diesel Fuel Estimator**: Calculates recommended fuel (e.g., 18L/ha) directly in the booking workflow.
- **Payment Method Choices**: Supports Cash-on-Dike and Co-op Passbook settlements.
- **Interactive Request Cards**: Live status tracking (*Driver En Route, Scheduled, Completed*) and driver contact details.
- **Verified RSBSA Identification Banner**: Displays official enrollment number and cooperative affiliation.

---

### 🚜 2. Machinery Provider & SACCO Depot Features & Updates
- **+ Post Tractor & Machinery Modal**: Allows providers to list tractors, harvesters, dryers, and transplanters with custom rates (`₱/ha`, `₱/day`, `₱/bag`) and fuel policies.
- **Backend Asset Integration**: Directly linked to `/api/assets` for persistent listing updates.
- **Live Farmer Needs Broadcast Feed**: Shows real-time machinery requests from local farmers with parcel size and urgency tags.
- **"Accept & Dispatch" Action**: 1-click button to accept farmer needs and generate field tickets.
- **High-Contrast Typography**: Header text updated to bright white for clear readability against dark green banners.

---

### 🔧 3. Field Mechanic & Mobile Repair Features & Updates
- **Emergency SOS Breakdown Feed**: Real-time broadcast for tractors and harvesters stuck in mud or facing mechanical failures.
- **GPS Coordinates & Dispatch Navigation**: Displays field dike landmarks and distance estimates for mobile repair vans.
- **Mobile Van Spare Parts Inventory**: Stock tracking for OEM V-belts, shear pins, filters, and hydraulic hoses with 1-click requisition.
- **Completed Repair Logging (`+ Log Completed Repair`)**: Records maintenance certificates, replaced parts, and diagnostic summaries.

---

### 🛡️ 4. Municipal Admin & MAO Features & Updates
- **Farmer ID Card System Creator**: Generates verified ID cards with high-density RSBSA barcodes (Front) and emergency/barangay contacts (Back).
- **CR80 PVC & Thermal ID Previews**: Standard printable card sizing.
- **Machinery Fleet Certification Audit Tab**: Validates engine numbers, serials, and DA-PhilMech safety clearances.
- **Municipal Dispatch & Settlement Ledger Tab**: Comprehensive audit logs for cash-on-dike and SACCO split receipts.

---

### 📄 5. Printable A4 Standards
- **Operator Field Dispatch Slip (`/dispatch-slip`)**: Fully formatted and calibrated for single-sheet standard A4 printing (`210mm x 297mm`).
- **SACCO Palay Harvest Split Scale Ticket (`/sacco-receipt`)**: Standardized A4 scale receipt with automatic 14.0% moisture content formulas, 8% harvester shares, and 2% drying fees.
- **Eco Thermal B&W Toggle**: High-contrast monochrome print mode for thermal slip printers.

---

### 🌐 6. Navigation & Global System Updates
- **Quick Role Switcher**: Instant switching between **Farmer**, **Provider**, **Mechanic**, and **Admin** workspaces inside the profile avatar menu.
- **Trilingual Language Selector**: Live instant switching between **English**, **Tagalog (Filipino)**, and **Bisaya (Cebuano)**.
- **Transferred Sign Out Action**: Moved cleanly into the profile avatar dropdown menu.
- **Clean Footer Layout**: Removed redundant offline badge text for a clean, government-compliant aesthetic.
