RSBSA Registration Sync

Provide your RSBSA (Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture) stub number. This instantly unlocks government subsidized rates set by the Department of Agriculture and eliminates commercial middleman markups.

Elderly or non-smartphone farmers can walk into their local Barangay Hall or FCA office. Your assigned Co-op Secretary will enter your request on the terminal and print your physical schedule slips.

Trust, Policies & Field Coordination FAQ

Learn how UMAKONEKTA eliminates middleman exploitation and keeps equipment dispatch accountable without digital payment risks.

Under DA-LGU Joint Circular No. 2024-03, agricultural machinery cooperatives and municipal dispatch desks must eliminate digital transaction gateway fees that disadvantage smallholder farmers. By enforcing cash-on-dike upon completed job sign-off or direct co-op member passbook ledger deduction, we eliminate electronic transaction charges, protect against cyber-phishing scams in rural areas, and maintain traditional agrarian trust practices.

When severe weather or heavy rains lodge standing rice paddies, the crop will ferment and spoil within 48 to 72 hours if not harvested immediately. Ticking the Urgent Spoilage Flag immediately elevates the request to the top of the Municipal Agriculture Officer (MAO) dispatch monitor. Dispatches are routed to nearest available combine harvesters within a 5-kilometer radius to minimize harvest loss.

Yes, absolutely. UMAKONEKTA was built with a mandatory Cooperative Proxy Account structure. Any farmer without an internet connection or smartphone can register their crop calendar at the Barangay Hall or Farmers' Cooperative (FCA) desk. The cooperative manager manages their bookings digitally on their behalf, and updates are forwarded via automatic basic SMS or physical notice board slips.

During Step 03 (Request Schedule), farmers are encouraged to upload a photo of the access canal or describe road access conditions (e.g. "accessible via 3-meter gravel irrigation canal road; bridge ramp required for rubber tracks"). Operators review this before acceptance, ensuring that suitable trailer ramps and equipment sizes are dispatched without getting stuck.

Reviews are tied directly to validated physical completion vouchers signed by both the farmer and the machine operator. After the job slip is recorded at the municipal desk, both parties receive a simple 1-to-5 star feedback prompt via low-data web or shortcode SMS. This prevents fake bot reviews and builds transparent, peer-verified reputation scores.
